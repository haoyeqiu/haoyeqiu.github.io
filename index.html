👨‍🎓 About Me

I am a second-year master's student at the PAttern Learning and Mining (PALM) Lab, School of Computer Science and Engineering, Southeast University (东南大学计算机科学与工程学院), China. My supervisor is Prof. Yuheng Jia (贾育衡), and my second supervisor is Assoc. Prof. Ding Ding (丁玎). I received my bachelor's degree from the School of Computer Science and Technology, Hainan University (海南大学计算机科学与技术学院).

My research focuses on theoretical computer science, learning theory, and machine learning. In particular, I am interested in clustering and focusing on:

  • Explainable clustering (k-medians, k-means, spectral clustering) — Current research focus
  • Ensemble clustering
  • Multi-view clustering
  • Dempster–Shafer evidence theory
  • Neutrosophic set theory
